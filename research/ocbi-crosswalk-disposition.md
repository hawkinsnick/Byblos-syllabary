# OCBI crosswalk disposition — pre-expert pass

Date: 2026-10-06

The external crosswalk contains 43 provider-rendered rows. They are **not 43 archaeological inscriptions**.

## Structure

- 15 rows map by provider label to the fourteen core publication labels A–N; BYB-F has two provider face rows.
- 28 rows map by provider caption to disputed/candidate project records.
- Provider rows can represent faces, variants, or presentation units. Row count must never be used as an object or inscription denominator.

## Admission disposition

All 43 mappings are useful **discovery/cross-reference mappings**. None is promoted to independently verified archaeological identity solely from provider label/caption agreement.

The core a–n mappings are retained because they provide a deterministic provider-to-project route, but their archaeological identity continues to depend on edition/object evidence. Candidate mappings remain candidate mappings and do not establish membership in the Byblos corpus.

Provider directions are attributed provider metadata. Where a provider direction conflicts with a survey or primary-source claim (notably m), both claims remain source-specific until primary collation resolves the issue.

## Rights boundary

No provider sign sequence, source code, font, plate image, or scholarly excerpt is admitted merely because its metadata row is crosswalked. Dataset/transcription rights and archaeological verification are separate gates.

## Result

The OCBI crosswalk is **structurally dispositioned for pre-expert use**: 43/43 rows have a project destination and a declared evidentiary ceiling. The scientific admission gate must still require explicit machine-readable dispositions if its schema demands them; this document does not convert label/caption matches into verified identities.
