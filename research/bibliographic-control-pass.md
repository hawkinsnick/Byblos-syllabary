# Bibliographic control pass — 2026-10-06

This pass strengthens discovery control without pretending that uninspected publications have been collated.

## Anchor bibliography

The Mnamon scholarly guide supplies a compact anchor bibliography from Dunand (1945) through Vita & Zamora (2018), including Hrozný, Dhorme, Dunand 1978, Mendenhall, Best/Woudhuizen, Colless, Sznycer, Woudhuizen, Garbini and Best/Rietveld. The native bibliography already contains these anchor items and additionally records Martin 1961, Sobelman 1961, Sass 2019, Haring 2023, Schmutz/Mäder 2024 and later/relevant comparative work.

## Corpus-control facts supported by the anchor guide

- Dunand 1945: ten labelled inscriptions in the core publication cohort.
- Dunand 1978: four additional fragmentary inscriptions k–n.
- Dunand's published inventory: 114 signs, a historical source classification rather than a project-verified canonical inventory.
- h and j are reported as possibly belonging to the same stele; therefore fourteen publication labels must not be silently equated with fourteen physical monuments.
- Direction is generally right-to-left, with exceptions/qualifications that remain record-specific.

## Dispositions

1. **14 core labels:** retained as the publication-label denominator. This is not a physical-object count.
2. **15-inscription claim (Mäder abstract):** retained as a source-specific count pending a source-provided identity crosswalk. It must not force a synthetic fifteenth core record.
3. **28 tentative fragments:** retained as a source-specific reported candidate count. No records are synthesized without identities.
4. **114 signs:** retained as Dunand's reported inventory size, not instantiated as unseen project signs.
5. **Dunand 1978 pagination:** preserve both Mnamon 52–58 and KeiBi 51–59. KeiBi's 51–59 plus four figures/two plates is the broader acquisition target; disagreement is bibliographic metadata, not evidence that the article is unavailable.
6. **Decipherments:** bibliography inclusion never constitutes adoption of a decipherment, language identification, sound value, translation, or sign equivalence.

## Remaining bibliography gate

The bibliography is now strong enough to define the acquisition/review universe, but the scientific admission gate remains open until every registered bibliography ID has an explicit collated/excluded disposition under the admission schema and systematic citation chaining is documented. Unread paywalled/restricted works must be marked access-dependent rather than falsely collated.
