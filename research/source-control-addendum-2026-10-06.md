# Source-control addendum — 2026-10-06

## Dunand 1978 locator

Three independently discoverable bibliographic witnesses are now preserved conceptually:

- Mnamon bibliography: BMB 30, pp. 52–58.
- KeiBi/Tübingen: BMB 30, pp. 51–59, 4 figures, 2 plates; nominal 1978, appeared 1981.
- Vita & Zamora 2018 bibliography: BMB 30, pp. 51–59.

**Disposition:** use pp. 51–59 as the acquisition/collation target because two witnesses support the broader range and KeiBi supplies figure/plate metadata. Preserve Mnamon's 52–58 as a source disagreement. This is a bibliographic reconciliation, not proof that the primary article has been collated.

## Historical sign-inventory assertions

Mnamon reports Dunand's 1945 inventory as 114 signs, with 74 in the two long bronze tablets and 30 additional signs in the remaining shorter/fragmentary inscriptions. Mnamon further reports that k–n add six new signs.

These are **source-reported historical inventory assertions only**. They must not instantiate six unseen signs, convert 114 into a project-verified canonical inventory, or erase graphic-variant uncertainty. Mnamon itself cautions that Dunand's 114 may not be complete and that some Dunand-distinct signs may be variants of one sign.

## Direction/layout control

Mnamon reports generally right-to-left writing, bidirectional behavior on some two-sided spatulae, and vertical-column layout on g. These claims support preserving surface-specific direction/layout rather than a corpus-wide direction default.
