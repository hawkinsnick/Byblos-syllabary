## Pre-expert conclusion

For A–J, substantial public/dependent evidence can constrain the review but cannot ethically substitute for full 1945 edition/plate collation. For K–N, the 1978 article/plates are the decisive primary acquisition target. The missing complete sequences are therefore increasingly concentrated at the **edition-access + epigraphic-collation boundary**, rather than being an unstructured engineering deficit.

Object identity remains a separate problem: publication labels, provider labels, surfaces, museum objects and physical monuments must not be collapsed without evidence.
