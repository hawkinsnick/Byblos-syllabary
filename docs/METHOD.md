# Method and evidence gates

## Foundation

The 0.1.0 catalogue records scholarly reports, not direct inspection of artifacts.
Fourteen labels are seeded from Mnamon; no unseen sign sequences are reconstructed.
The 15-inscription and 28-fragment claims from Mäder are coverage leads. They are
not automatically added to produce a total. Sources may count different units.

## Next milestones

| Stage | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| 0.2 | Complete bibliographic leads; inspect original editions; document search queries, dates and inclusion criteria |
| 0.3 | Reconcile physical objects, inscription labels and surfaces; identify proposed fragments individually |
| 0.4 | Establish sourced sign inventories and competing variant classifications; preserve edition numbering |
| 0.5 | Import or independently prepare transcriptions with per-occurrence uncertainty and asset rights |
| 1.0 | Audit coverage against a dated inventory; reproduce analyses; resolve or explicitly document all gates; independent specialist review |

These are evidence targets, not promises about corpus size or dates.

## Data expansion

An inscription ID must not stand in for a museum accession or a surface ID.
Future editions attach to inscriptions without overwriting earlier sequences.
Each sign occurrence will retain surface, line, position, reading status, edition,
source locator, and alternatives. Unknown, damaged, reconstructed and absent are
different states. Date intervals must carry their proposer and evidential basis.

Sign equivalences are attributed relationships, not universal truths. A phonetic
value requires a named decipherment proposal. Normalization must be reversible.
Sequence statistics must report edition choice, damage handling, corpus subset,
direction assumptions and sensitivity to alternate sign classifications.

## Exhaustiveness

Track the searched bibliography, unexamined publications, inaccessible sources,
disputed members and rejected candidates. Exhaustiveness can only be claimed
relative to a dated, specified inventory. Newly discovered material can reopen
a closed gate. Structural validation cannot certify scholarship.

## Review and rights

Reviewers must be identified with consent; log their scope and corrections.
No correspondence has been sent and no permission has been requested yet.
Log rights independently for photographs, drawings, fonts, transcriptions,
bibliographic metadata and original code. An article's licence need not cover
external datasets it describes.

## Relationship to sister projects

Reuse a common envelope for stable IDs, provenance, versioning, uncertainty,
rights and release gates. Keep Byblos sign identities and linguistic hypotheses
local. Inspect sibling schemas before declaring interoperable exports.

## Prerelease implementation status

Versions 0.2–0.6 denote tested development snapshots, not closure of the scientific
milestones above. Source discovery, candidate cataloguing, validation and exports
can progress while source access, epigraphy, rights and independent review remain
open. The latest audit is the authority for release readiness.

Do not relabel an infrastructure snapshot as a scientifically complete 1.0.
The pipeline must return exit status 2 for an attempted scientific 1.0 check until
its evidence gates pass. A blocked audit is an intended result, not a broken test.

Scientific admission now uses the tested policy in ADMISSION_POLICY.md. Its positive fixtures are synthetic; they do not establish review or complete coverage of real inscriptions.
